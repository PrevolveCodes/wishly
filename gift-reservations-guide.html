How gift reservations work

A wishlist link lets anyone browse. An invitation lets a gift-giver see what’s available and reserve an item. Each invitation is for one wishlist, so there’s no family group to join.

  1. 1. Choose a gift organizer

    Open your wishlist from My Lists. Under Sharing options, choose Invite gift-givers. Enter a trusted person’s email, then open the email draft and send them the link. You’ll still be able to edit your list while reservation activity stays hidden from your account.

  2. 2. Invite gift-givers

    Open the wishlist link, sign in with the invited email, and choose Accept organizer invitation. Add each gift-giver’s email, then use Open email draft to send them the link.

  3. 3. Reserve a gift

    Open your invitation and sign in with the email your organizer used. Verify your email if prompted. Choose Reserve gift on an available item. Other gift-givers will see that it’s taken. Use Undo reservation if you change your mind.
